# Scroll to Top
<p>Theme options > Footer > Scroll to Top – From this tab, we can Customize Scroll to Top Options.</p>
<ul>
    <li>Show/ hide Scroll to Top.</li>
    <li>Set Scroll to Top border-radius.</li>
    <li>Customize Scroll to Top background color and border-color property.</li>
    <li>Set Scroll to Top icon color and hover color.</li>
</ul>
